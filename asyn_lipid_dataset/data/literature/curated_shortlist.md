# Hand-read shortlist — papers that genuinely test lipid × α-synuclein aggregation

Verdicts from **reading abstracts** (not keyword scores), after a systematic search (384) +
citation-snowball (256) = **640 relevant papers**, of which **~280 full texts are already
fetched here**. Below are the papers that actually test a **lipid** (ideally one of the 8
candidates) on α-synuclein **aggregation/fibrillation**. `HAVE` = full text already fetched
(I can extract it now); `GET` = paywalled, worth your download.

## By candidate

### GM1 (ganglioside) — well covered
- `HAVE` Grey 2015 (PMC4317028) & Gaspar 2018 (PMC6121081) — already in the dataset.
- `HAVE` **GM1 oligosaccharide efficacy against α-syn aggregation & toxicity in vitro**, PMC10579883
  (2023) — GM1-OS **inhibits** aggregation. Extract-ready, inhibition direction.
- `HAVE` Transient lipid-protein structures / ganglioside co-aggregation, PMC7930334 (2021).
- `HAVE` N-α-acetylation increases GM1 binding specificity & aggregation resistance, PMC4116227 (2014).
- `GET` **GM1 and OligoGM1 Drive Divergent α-Synuclein Aggregation Pathways** — Langmuir 2025,
  doi:10.1021/acs.langmuir.5c04731 (ThT, GM1). High value.
- `GET` **GM1 ganglioside role in α-syn–membrane interaction: morphology & structure** — Biophys Chem
  2019, doi:10.1016/j.bpc.2019.106272.
- `GET` **Martinez 2007** full text + SI, doi:10.1021/bi061749a (the GM1-inhibition numbers).

### Cholesterol — now well covered (was 1 paper)
- `HAVE` Matveyenka 2024 (PMC11587506) — already in the dataset (accelerates at 60%).
- `HAVE` **Phospholipids and Cholesterol Determine Cytotoxicity of α-Syn Oligomers/Fibrils**,
  PMC12152213 (2024, Kurouski group) — PS/cholesterol, aggregation + toxicity.
- `HAVE` **Familial mutations modulate α-syn interactions with key neuronal membrane lipids**
  (cholesterol, SM), PMC12927006 (2026).
- `GET` **Zwitterionic nanoliposomes + cholesterol *retard* α-syn fibrillization** — Nanoscale 2018,
  doi:10.1039/c8nr00632f (ThT lag/elongation; **inhibition** direction). Top value.
- `GET` **Cholesterol-containing lipid nanodiscs accelerate oligomerization** (cholesterol+DOPC),
  FEBS J 2021, doi:10.1111/febs.15551 (ThT).
- `GET` **Cholesterol in SV membranes regulates vesicle-binding, function & aggregation of α-syn**,
  J Phys Chem B 2021, doi:10.1021/acs.jpcb.1c03533.
- `GET` **Oxidized cholesterol metabolites accelerate α-synuclein fibrilization** — Nat Chem Biol 2006,
  doi:10.1038/nchembio782 (oxysterols; disease-brain relevant).

### DOPC
- `HAVE` **Dual Effects of Presynaptic Membrane Mimetics (DOPC/DOPS) on α-Syn Amyloid Aggregation**,
  PMC9209734 (2022). Extract-ready.
- `GET` **Electrostatic Regulation of α-Syn Membrane Binding ↔ Aggregation Kinetics** (DOPC/DOPA),
  JACS 2026, doi:10.1021/jacs.6c08246.

### POPC — was ZERO; now has evidence
- `HAVE` **Assembly of α-synuclein aggregates on phospholipid bilayers** (POPC), PMC6661114 (2019).
- `GET` **α-Synuclein interaction with POPC/POPS vesicles** — Soft Matter 2025, doi:10.1039/d4sm01036a
  (adsorption vs anionic fraction).

### DMPC / DPPC / DSPC
- `GET` **Dou, Matveyenka, Kurouski 2023** (DMPC suppresses; PC vs PS), doi:10.1021/acschemneuro.3c00314.
- DSPC: still **no direct in-vitro aggregation study found** — a genuine gap / experiment to run.

### Sphingomyelin
- `HAVE` PMC12927006 (cholesterol + SM, familial mutations). Most other SM hits are cellular/lipidomics.

## Classic lipid-aggregation references (not candidate-specific but foundational)
- `GET` **Lipid binding inhibits α-synuclein fibril formation** — JBC 2003, doi:10.1074/jbc.m210136200.
- `GET` **Rapid anionic micelle-mediated α-syn fibrillization** — JBC 2003, doi:10.1074/jbc.m308231200.
- `GET` **Association of α-syn with membranes affects bilayer structure & fibril formation** — JBC 2003,
  doi:10.1074/jbc.m305326200.
- `GET` **α-Syn membrane interactions and lipid specificity** — JBC 2000, doi:10.1074/jbc.m004345200.
- `GET` **Methyl-branched lipids promote adsorption via packing defects** — PCCP 2015,
  doi:10.1039/c5cp00244c (supports the packing-defect rule).

## Explicitly NOT relevant (read and excluded — examples)
Lipidomics/observational (Dinter 2026; spatial-lipidomics 2026; Raman lipid changes), cellular/in-vivo
(GBA/Gaucher fibroblasts, Lovastatin/statin mouse & cell models, mice lacking gangliosides, Sandhoff,
FTY720, APOE4 miBrains, C. elegans), non-lipid modulators (serum albumin, dopamine/DOPAC, β-synuclein,
Hsp90, proSAAS, SS-31 & other peptides), different proteins (apoC-II, butyrylcholinesterase, TMEM106B),
reviews (Uversky; "Gangliosides, α-Syn & PD"; LRP1; sphingolipids-in-AD), and keyword false-positives
(PDC-109 bovine seminal plasma; copper toxicology; manganese/APLP1).
