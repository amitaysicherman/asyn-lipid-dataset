# Hand-read shortlist — papers that genuinely test lipid × α-synuclein aggregation

These verdicts come from **reading the abstracts**, not keyword scores. Scope: an in-vitro
experiment where a **lipid** (ideally one of the 8 candidates, or a close analogue) changes
α-synuclein **aggregation** (ThT / fibrillation kinetics). Binding-only, structural-only,
chaperone/peptide/small-molecule inhibitors, reviews, lipidomics and cell/animal models are
marked separately.

## TOP priority to download (paywalled, directly on-topic)
1. **Cholesterol nanoliposomes retard α-syn fibrillization** — *Nanoscale* 2018,
   doi:10.1039/c8nr00632f. Neutral (zwitterionic) nanoliposomes ± **cholesterol** + PEG
   **retard** αSN fibrillization (increase lag time). Candidate lipid, **inhibition** direction,
   ThT lag/elongation split. → directly tests a candidate; one of the few true inhibition papers.
2. **Lipid binding inhibits α-synuclein fibril formation** — *JBC* 2003 (Zhu & Fink),
   doi:10.1074/jbc.m210136200. Vesicles of varied composition/size; fibrillation kinetics depend
   on phospholipid composition and lipid:protein ratio. The classic lipid-inhibition reference.
3. **Electrostatic regulation of α-syn membrane binding couples to aggregation kinetics** —
   *JACS* 2026, doi:10.1021/jacs.6c08246. **DOPC**/DOPA proteoliposomes, MAS-NMR + **ThT** + TEM;
   membrane charge vs aggregation rate. Candidate (DOPC).
4. **Rapid anionic micelle-mediated α-syn fibrillization** — *JBC* 2003,
   doi:10.1074/jbc.m308231200. Fatty acids / anionic detergents accelerate fibrillization;
   explicit lag times (as short as 11 min) and growth-rate constants. Quantitative.
5. **Association of α-syn with membranes affects bilayer structure, stability & fibril
   formation** — *JBC* 2003, doi:10.1074/jbc.m305326200. Bilayers of different compositions.
6. **Structural & aggregation properties linked to phospholipase A2 / arachidonic acid** —
   *Protein Pept Lett* 2018, doi:10.2174/0929866525666180326120052. Polyunsaturated **fatty acid**
   (arachidonic) induces αS conformational change + fibrillation.
7. **α-Syn membrane interactions and lipid specificity** — *JBC* 2000,
   doi:10.1074/jbc.m004345200. Binding to acidic phospholipids, augmented by PE (mostly
   binding/CD — supporting, not primary kinetics).

## Read but NOT relevant to the lipid-aggregation question (reasons)
- *PDC-109, bovine seminal plasma chaperone* (JBC 2010) — about a **sperm protein**; α-syn only
  named as an analogy. (keyword false-positive on "cholesterol")
- *Serum albumin impedes amyloid aggregation* (BBA 2018) — inhibitor is a **protein**, not a lipid.
- *Dopamine / DOPAC / dequalinium / β-synuclein / Hsp90 / proSAAS / N-WASP / SS-31 / stapled &
  library peptides* — **non-lipid** modulators (small molecules, chaperones, peptides).
- *SARS-CoV-2 spike fragment* — protein cross-seeding, not lipid.
- *Lewy-body growth models, yeast/worm/mouse models, splicing variants, 19F-NMR, MD-simulation
  structure, organic-solvent models* — modeling / cellular / structural, no lipid-aggregation assay.
- *Uversky review; "Factors responsible…" review; Lewy-body reviews* — **reviews** (reference-
  mining only).
